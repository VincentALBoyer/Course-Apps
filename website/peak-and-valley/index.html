⛰️ Peak and Valley

Exhaustive vs. local search · 5 rounds

Each round hides a function f(x) behind a wall of unrevealed points. Find its lowest value while revealing as few points as you can. The search space grows every round, just like the combinatorial explosion we saw in class.

How it works

  1. 🙈 Hidden landscape Click a point on the chart to evaluate f(x) there. Unrevealed points sit on the shelf at the bottom.
  2. 📈 Growing search space Round 1 has 11 points, round 5 has 31. Checking everything gets more expensive each round.
  3. 🎯 One strategy You choose your strategy once and play all 5 rounds with it. Compare with classmates who chose differently.

How scoring works

Quality 65%Efficiency 35%

Each round is scored out of 1000: 65% quality (how close you get to the true lowest value) plus 35% efficiency (how few points you reveal). Local search and greedy can beat exhaustive's fixed score by converging fast, or fall short if they land poorly.
