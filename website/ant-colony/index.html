🐜 Ant Colony: The Pheromone Trail

Ant colony optimization · explore, deposit, evaporate

Find the shortest tour that visits every city once and returns home. No single ant is smart: each one picks its next city at random, favoring cities that are close and edges with a lot of pheromone. Short tours get more pheromone, so over the iterations the colony's trail converges on a good tour.

How it works

  1. 🎲 Build a tour Each ant starts at a random city and picks the next one with probability ∝ τα · ηβ.
  2. 📏 η: distance η = 1/d: closer cities are more attractive. β sets how much that matters.
  3. 🟠 τ: pheromone The colony's memory of good edges. α sets how much the ants trust it.
  4. 💨 Evaporate & deposit After each iteration, a share ρ of all pheromone evaporates, then each ant lays Q / L on its tour.
